01 / About

The fine margins
shape the whole game.

A professional cricket coach specialising in fielding excellence, agility and performance development — with more than 18 years of progressive coaching experience from grassroots to high-performance cricket.

Manikantan designs fielding drills, reflex training, agility exercises and precision-based coordination programmes to improve technical proficiency, athletic performance and match readiness.

His coaching approach centres on technically sound, mentally resilient, disciplined and team-oriented cricketers — supported by a culture of continuous improvement and competitive excellence.

Currently Director / Head Cricket Coach Peak Sports Academy · Abu Dhabi May 2024 — Present
